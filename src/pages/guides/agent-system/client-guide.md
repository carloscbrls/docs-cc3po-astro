# Getting Started With Your AI Assistant

Your AI assistant is already set up and running. This guide helps you connect to it so you can start giving it tasks.

**Time needed:** 5 minutes
**What you need:** Your phone with Telegram installed

---

## Step 1: Install Telegram

If you don't have Telegram yet:

1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android)
2. Search for **Telegram**
3. Download and open it
4. Create your account with your phone number

> 💡 Telegram is like WhatsApp but more secure. We use it because your AI assistant lives there — you just send a message and it responds.

---

## Step 2: Connect to Your Assistant

During onboarding, we created a private Telegram bot just for you. Here's how to find it:

1. Open Telegram
2. Tap the **search icon** (magnifying glass) at the top
3. Type your bot name: **@yourbusiness_bot** (we gave you this name in your welcome email)
4. Tap on the bot in the search results
5. Tap **Start**

That's it. Your assistant is now connected.

> 🔒 **Only you** can talk to your bot. It's private — no one else sees your conversations.

---

## Step 3: Say Hello

Send your assistant a message:

> "Hello! What can you do?"

Your assistant will respond with a list of things it can help with. Try these:

| Say This | What Happens |
|----------|-------------|
| "How many leads came in today?" | Shows your latest leads |
| "What's my website status?" | Checks your site is online |
| "Post to Facebook: Spring is here! Book your AC tune-up now." | Posts to your connected social media |
| "Send a follow-up to the last lead" | Sends an email follow-up |
| "What's on my schedule?" | Shows upcoming tasks and posts |
| "Create a blog post about [topic]" | Drafts a blog post for your review |

---

## Step 4: Web Dashboard (Optional)

You also have a web dashboard where you can see everything at a glance:

1. Open your browser
2. Go to: **http://your-server-ip:4080** (we gave you this in your welcome email)
3. Enter your auth token (also in your welcome email)

**What you'll see:**

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Status** | Is your system running ✅ or has an issue ❌ |
| **Activity** | Recent actions your assistant took |
| **Cron Jobs** | Scheduled tasks (social posts, monitoring, etc.) |
| **Messages** | Conversation history with your assistant |

You don't need to use the dashboard — Telegram is enough for most things. But it's useful if you want to see the big picture.

## How to Request Tasks

### The Easy Way: Just Tell Your Assistant

Send a message in Telegram like you're talking to a person:

> "Hey, can you post something on Instagram about our new service?"
> "Check if my website is loading fast"
> "Send me my monthly report"
> "Create a promotion for Father's Day"

### Tips for Best Results

**Be specific:**
- ❌ "Do marketing" → Too vague
- ✅ "Create 3 Instagram posts about spring HVAC maintenance tips for homeowners"

**Give context:**
- ✅ "Our audience is homeowners in Sacramento, keep it casual and friendly"

**Say what you want:**
- ✅ "Post this to Facebook and Instagram: [your message]"

**Ask questions:**
- ✅ "What should I post about this week?" → Your assistant will suggest ideas

## Common Questions

### "What if my assistant stops responding?"
1. Check your internet connection
2. Close and reopen Telegram
3. Send "hello" again
4. If still no response, email support@cc3po.com — we'll fix it within 4 hours

### "Can my assistant make decisions for me?"
- Routine tasks (posting, following up, monitoring): **Yes, automatically**
- Important decisions (pricing, contracts, hiring): **No, it will ask you first**
- Financial actions: **Never without your approval**

### "Is my data safe?"
Yes. Your data lives on YOUR own private server. No other client can access it. We (CC3PO) can access it only for maintenance, and we never share it.

### "Can I pause my assistant?"
Yes. Just tell it: "Pause all posting for this week" or "Stop lead research until I say otherwise." You can also contact us to pause your subscription.

### "What if I want something changed?"
Just tell your assistant! "Change the phone number on my website to 555-1234" or "Update my business hours to 9-6 weekdays." For bigger changes (new pages, redesign), email support@cc3po.com.

### "How do I cancel?"
Email support@cc3po.com with 30 days notice. We'll help you export your data and transition out smoothly. No hassle.
