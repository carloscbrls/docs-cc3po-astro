# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to know about working with your CC3PO AI assistant.

## General

### What exactly is an AI assistant?

An AI assistant is a software system powered by artificial intelligence that handles tasks for your business — posting to social media, monitoring your website, finding leads, updating content, and responding to routine inquiries. It works 24/7 and never calls in sick.

Think of it as a digital employee that handles the repetitive stuff so you can focus on what matters most: your customers and your business.

### Do I need to be technical?

**No.** That's the whole point. You interact with your assistant through simple messages in Telegram — plain English, no coding required. Just say what you need:

> "Post about our summer sale on Facebook"
> "Change our business hours to 9–6"
> "Show me this week's leads"

Your assistant handles the rest.

### Can I see what my assistant is doing?

**Yes.** Full transparency. You can ask your assistant at any time:

> "What did you do today?"
> "Show me this week's social posts"
> "What's the status of my website?"

You also get a monthly activity summary with all actions taken.

### What if I don't like the content it creates?

Tell your assistant! It learns from your feedback:

> "That post was too formal — keep it casual"
> "Don't use emojis in LinkedIn posts"
> "Always mention our family-owned status"

The more feedback you give, the better your assistant gets at matching your brand voice.

## Security & Data

### Is my data safe?

Yes. Your website runs on its own private server — no shared hosting. Your data is isolated, encrypted, and monitored 24/7. We never share your data with other clients or third parties. See our [Security & Privacy](/guides/agent-system/security) guide for full details.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. We require 30 days written notice. Email support@cc3po.com with "Cancellation Request" in the subject. Service continues through your paid period. See [Billing & Payments](/guides/agent-system/payments) for details.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

- Your website data is exported and provided to you
- Your social media accounts remain yours — we disconnect our access
- All your data is deleted from our systems within 30 days
- You keep full ownership of everything you created

## Service Details

### How fast does it respond?

Most requests are handled **within minutes** during business hours. Routine tasks like social media posting happen automatically. Urgent requests (website down, security concern) are prioritized immediately.

### Can it make decisions without me?

**Only if you want it to.** By default, your assistant asks before taking significant actions. You can enable auto-approval for routine tasks:

- ✅ Auto-approve: social media posts that match your content calendar
- ✅ Auto-approve: routine website updates you've requested
- ❌ Always ask: new leads, financial decisions, anything public-facing

You're always in control.
