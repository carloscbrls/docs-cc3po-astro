# Lead Generation

Your AI assistant actively finds potential customers for your business — every single day. Here's how it works.

## How It Works

1. **Research** — Your assistant searches for businesses matching your ideal customer profile
2. **Qualify** — It filters leads based on your criteria (industry, location, size, needs)
3. **Summarize** — You receive a clean summary with key details
4. **Follow up** — Your assistant can send initial outreach on your behalf

## Daily Lead Research

Your assistant researches potential customers in your area **every day**. It finds:

- Business names and contact information
- Industry and services offered
- Online presence (website quality, social activity)
- Identified opportunities (poor website, no SEO, outdated info)

You don't have to search for leads. They come to you.

## Reviewing Leads

Just ask your assistant:

> "Show me today's leads"
> "How many leads did we get this week?"
> "Show me HVAC companies near Sacramento"

You'll get a clean summary with:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Business Name | ABC Heating & Air |
| Contact | John Smith, Owner |
| Phone | (555) 123-4567 |
| Website | abcheating.com |
| Opportunity | Outdated website, no online booking |
| Priority | ⭐⭐⭐ High |

## Following Up

Your assistant can handle outreach:

> "Send a follow-up to the last lead"
> "Email ABC Heating about their website"
> "Send the intro email to all this week's leads"

All outreach uses **your** email and brand voice. You review and approve before anything is sent (unless you've told your assistant to send automatically).

## What Makes a Good Lead

Your assistant looks for businesses that match your **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**:

- In your service area
- In your target industries
- Showing signs they need your help (poor online presence, outdated website, bad reviews)
- Small to medium size (your sweet spot)

You can customize this at any time. For example:

> "Only show me HVAC companies within 50 miles of Sacramento"
> "Focus on dental practices, not general businesses"
> "Skip companies that already have a modern website"

## Lead Quality Scoring

Each lead gets a quality score based on:

- **Fit** — How well they match your ICP
- **Intent** — Signs they need your services
- **Accessibility** — Can you reach them?
- **Value** — Potential revenue opportunity

High-scoring leads are flagged for priority follow-up.

## Lead Pipeline Stages

| Stage | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| 🔍 New | Just discovered, not yet contacted |
| 📧 Contacted | Initial outreach sent |
| 💬 Responded | They replied — hot lead |
| 📅 Scheduled | Meeting or demo booked |
| ✅ Closed | They became a client |
| ❌ Lost | Not interested or unreachable |

Ask your assistant for a pipeline summary anytime:

> "Show me my lead pipeline"
> "How many leads are in the responded stage?"

## Tips for Better Lead Generation

1. **Be specific** — Tell your assistant exactly who you're looking for
2. **Review leads daily** — Fresh leads convert better
3. **Follow up quickly** — Your assistant can send initial outreach within minutes
4. **Refine your ICP** — The more specific your ideal customer, the better your leads
5. **Share what works** — Tell your assistant which leads turned into clients so it can find more like them
