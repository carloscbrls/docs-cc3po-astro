# Billing & Payments

Simple, transparent pricing. No hidden fees. Here's how billing works.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly Price | Best For |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Starter** | $297/mo | Solo businesses getting started |
| **Business** | $497/mo | Growing businesses that need more |
| **Enterprise** | $997/mo | Multi-location or complex operations |

Each plan includes your AI assistant, website management, monitoring, and monthly content updates.

## Setup Fee

There is a **one-time setup fee** at the start of your service. This covers:

- Server provisioning and configuration
- AI assistant setup and training
- Social media account connections
- Initial content creation

The setup fee is **non-refundable** after work begins.

## Monthly Billing

- **Auto-billed via Stripe** on the same date each month as your signup
- **Your Stripe account** — Payments go directly to your bank, not through us
- **No surprises** — Same amount every month unless you add services

## Add-On Services

| Service | Price | Details |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Social Media Add-On | $97/mo | Zernio setup, 3–5 posts/week, content calendar |
| Additional Website Changes | $75/change | After your 2 included monthly changes |
| New Website Page | $150/page | Custom-designed, fully responsive |
| Major Website Redesign | $500+ | Quoted individually based on scope |

## Updating Your Payment Method

Email **support@cc3po.com** with the subject "Update Payment Method" and we'll send you a secure Stripe link to update your card or bank information.

## What Happens If Payment Fails

1. **We retry in 3 days** — No interruption to your service
2. **We email you** — With instructions to update your payment method
3. **7 days after first failure** — If still unpaid, service may be paused
4. **No late fees** — We just need a working payment method on file

## Cancellation

- **30 days written notice** required
- Email **support@cc3po.com** with "Cancellation Request" in the subject
- Service continues through your paid period
- No early termination fees

## Refund Policy

| Item | Policy |
|------|--------|
| Setup fee | Non-refundable after work begins |
| Monthly fee | Prorated refund for unused days |
| Add-on services | Prorated if not yet delivered |

## Billing Date

Your billing date is the **same day each month** as your original signup date. For example:

- Signed up on the 15th? You're billed on the 15th every month
- Signed up on the 1st? You're billed on the 1st every month

Need to change your billing date? Email support@cc3po.com and we'll adjust it.

## Invoice Requests

All invoices are available in your Stripe dashboard. If you need a custom invoice, just ask your assistant or email support@cc3po.com.
