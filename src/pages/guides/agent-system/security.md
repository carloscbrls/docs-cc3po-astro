# Security & Privacy

Your security is our top priority. Here's how we protect your business, data, and customers.

## Your Server Security

Your website runs on its own **private, isolated server**:

- **Dedicated firewall** — Blocks unauthorized access before it reaches your site
- **Fail2ban** — Automatically bans IPs that show malicious behavior (brute force, spam, etc.)
- **Auto-updates** — WordPress core, plugins, and themes are updated automatically with safety checks
- **Daily backups** — Your data is backed up daily with 30-day retention

No other clients share your server. Your data is completely isolated.

## SSL & Encryption

- **Let's Encrypt SSL** — Industry-standard HTTPS encryption for your website
- **Auto-renewing** — Certificates renew before expiration, no manual action needed
- **Always active** — Visitors always see the secure padlock in their browser
- **Included in your plan** — No extra charge for SSL

## Data Privacy

### Your Data Is Yours

- **We never share your data** with other clients, third parties, or advertisers
- **We access your server only for maintenance** — scheduled updates, security patches, and resolving issues you report
- **You can request a data export** at any time
- **You can request deletion** at any time (subject to billing obligations)

### HIPAA Compliance

For dental and healthcare clients:

- **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** available upon request
- **Encrypted data at rest and in transit**
- **Access controls** — Role-based permissions, no shared credentials
- **Audit logging** — All access is logged and reviewable
- **HIPAA-compliant hosting** — Infrastructure meets HIPAA requirements

Ask about our HIPAA Health Scanner for a full compliance assessment.

## Your AI Assistant & Privacy

Your AI assistant:

- **Never posts without your approval** (unless you've explicitly enabled auto-posting)
- **Never shares your data** with other clients or third parties
- **Only accesses what it needs** to do its job
- **Operates within rules you set** — You define what it can and can't do
- **All interactions are logged** — You can review what your assistant has done at any time

## If You Suspect a Breach

**Act fast:**

1. **Email support@cc3po.com with URGENT in the subject line**
2. We respond within 1 hour during business hours
3. We investigate immediately
4. You receive a full incident report

What we'll do:

- Isolate the affected system
- Identify what was accessed
- Patch the vulnerability
- Restore from backup if needed
- Provide a detailed report

## Security Best Practices for You

1. **Use strong passwords** — Your assistant can help generate and manage these
2. **Enable two-factor authentication** — We set this up during onboarding
3. **Don't share login credentials** — Each team member gets their own access
4. **Report anything suspicious** — Email support@cc3po.com immediately
5. **Keep your contact info updated** — So we can reach you in an emergency
