# Social Media Automation

Your AI assistant handles your social media so you can focus on running your business. Here's how it works.

## Supported Platforms

| Platform | Status | Best For |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Facebook | ✅ Active | Local business, community |
| Instagram | ✅ Active | Visual content, stories |
| LinkedIn | ✅ Active | B2B, professional services |
| Twitter/X | ✅ Active | Quick updates, industry news |
| Threads | ✅ Active | Text-based conversations |
| TikTok | ✅ Active | Short video, younger audiences |
| YouTube | ✅ Active | Long-form video, tutorials |
| Google Business | ✅ Active | Local SEO, reviews |

## How It Works

### Zernio Integration

We use **Zernio** — a unified social media platform — to manage all your accounts from one place.

1. **You get your own Zernio account** — Your social accounts are connected to your own instance, not shared with anyone else
2. **We handle setup** — During onboarding, we connect all your social platforms to Zernio
3. **Your assistant posts automatically** — No logging in, no scheduling tools to learn

### Automatic Posting

Your assistant posts **3–5 times per week** based on your business type:

- **Local service businesses:** Tips, before/after, community content, seasonal reminders
- **Professional services:** Industry insights, how-tos, thought leadership
- **Retail:** Product spotlights, promotions, customer highlights

Posts are tailored to your brand voice and industry. Your assistant learns what works and adjusts over time.

## Requesting a Specific Post

Just tell your assistant in Telegram:

> "Post about our new service on Facebook and Instagram"
> "Share this customer review on LinkedIn"
> "Announce our weekend sale on all platforms"

Your assistant will draft the post, show it to you for approval (if you prefer), and publish it.

## Content Calendar

Every month, your assistant creates a **content calendar** with planned posts:

- Topics and themes for each week
- Which platforms each post goes to
- Best posting times per platform
- You review and approve — or request changes

You always have final say on what gets posted.

## Pause or Resume Posting

Need a break? Just say:

> "Pause all social posts for the next week"

Or be specific:

> "Pause Facebook posts but keep LinkedIn going"

To resume:

> "Resume all social posts"

## Reconnecting Expired Platforms

Social platform connections sometimes expire (typically every 60–90 days for security). If you see a notice that a platform needs reconnection:

1. **Email support@cc3po.com** with the subject "Reconnect [Platform Name]"
2. We'll send you a secure link to reconnect
3. Your assistant resumes posting once reconnected

This is normal — it's a security feature, not a bug.

## Tips for Great Social Content

- **Share real stories** — Customers connect with authenticity
- **Include photos** — Posts with images get 2–3x more engagement
- **Be consistent** — Your assistant handles this, but feed it good material
- **Respond to comments** — Let your assistant know about important conversations
