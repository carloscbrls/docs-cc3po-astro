# Your Website

Your website runs on a private, secure server — monitored and maintained 24/7 by your AI assistant. Here's what that means for you.

## What Your Assistant Monitors

| What | How Often | What It Means |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Uptime | Every 60 seconds | Is your site online and responding? |
| SSL Certificate | Daily | Is your HTTPS security active? |
| Security Threats | Real-time | Are there attack attempts? |
| Performance | Every 5 minutes | Is your site loading fast? |
| Software Updates | As released | Are plugins and themes current? |
| Backups | Daily | Is your data safely stored? |

If anything goes wrong, your assistant is already on it — often before you even notice.

## How SSL Works

Your site uses **Let's Encrypt SSL certificates** — the industry standard for HTTPS encryption.

- **Auto-renews** before expiration — no action needed from you
- **Always active** — visitors see the secure padlock in their browser
- **No extra charge** — SSL is included in your plan

You never have to think about SSL. It just works.

## What To Do If Your Site Goes Down

**Short answer: Nothing.**

If your site goes down:

1. Your assistant detects it within 60 seconds
2. It starts investigating and fixing immediately
3. You get a notification about what happened and what's being done
4. Once restored, you get a follow-up

You're welcome to check in, but you don't need to take action. We're probably already fixing it.

- **Monitored** — 24/7 uptime, performance, and security monitoring
- **Backed up** — Daily backups with 30-day retention

### What's Included
