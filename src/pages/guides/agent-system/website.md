# Your Website

Your website runs on a private, secure server — monitored and maintained 24/7 by your AI assistant. Think of it like having a full-time web developer who never sleeps.

> 💡 **In simple terms:** You don't need to worry about your website. Your AI assistant watches it, fixes problems, and makes updates — all you do is ask.

## What Your Assistant Monitors

Your assistant keeps an eye on your website around the clock. Here's what it watches:

| What | How Often | What It Means for You |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| 🟢 Uptime | Every 60 seconds | Makes sure your site is online and working |
| 🔒 SSL Certificate | Daily | Keeps the "secure" padlock showing in browsers |
| 🛡️ Security Threats | Real-time | Blocks hackers and suspicious activity |
| ⚡ Performance | Every 5 minutes | Makes sure your site loads fast |
| 🔄 Software Updates | As released | Keeps WordPress, plugins, and themes current |

## How SSL Works (The "Secure" Lock)

Your site uses **Let's Encrypt SSL certificates** — the industry standard for website security. Here's what that means for you:

- ✅ **Auto-renews** — it never expires, no action needed from you
- ✅ **Always active** — visitors always see the secure padlock in their browser
- ✅ **No extra charge** — SSL is included in your plan, free

You never have to think about SSL. It just works.
