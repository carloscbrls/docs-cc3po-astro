## Shield — $97/month

**For small businesses that need to know where they stand.**

Shield gives you a clear picture of your compliance health every month. Think of it as a regular checkup — we scan your site, find the issues, and hand you a report so you can take action.

### What's Included

- **WCAG Compliance Scan** — We check your website against WCAG 2.1 AA standards (the most common accessibility benchmark referenced in ADA lawsuits). You'll see exactly which pages have issues and what they are.
- **Privacy Policy Audit** — We review your privacy policy for gaps against CCPA, GDPR, and state-level privacy laws. If it's missing required disclosures, you'll know.
- **Cookie & Tracker Detection** — We scan for cookies, tracking pixels, and third-party scripts on your site. Know what's tracking your visitors — before a regulator asks.
- **SEO Basics Check** — Missing meta descriptions? Broken headings? Duplicate content? We flag the SEO fundamentals that affect your search rankings.
- **Security Headers Audit** — We check for essential security headers like Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, and Strict-Transport-Security. Missing headers are an open door for certain attacks.
- **Monthly Reports** — A clean, readable report delivered to your inbox every month. No jargon, just what's wrong and what to do about it.

### Who Shield Is For

- Small businesses with fewer than 50 pages
- Startups that need a baseline compliance check
- Anyone who wants to understand their exposure before committing to more
- Businesses that have someone in-house who can fix issues once they're identified

### What Shield Doesn't Do

Shield finds problems — it doesn't fix them. If you want CC3PO to automatically generate fix patches, alert you the moment something breaks, or provide priority support, look at Fortress or Citadel.

## Fortress — $247/month

**For growing businesses that want problems found **and** fixed.**

Fortress is our most popular plan. It takes everything Shield does and adds the two things business owners ask for most: **automatic fixes** and **faster alerts**.

### What's Included (Everything in Shield, Plus)

- **Auto-Fix Code Patches** — When we find a compliance issue that can be fixed with a code change (missing alt text, broken ARIA labels, incorrect heading hierarchy, missing security headers), we generate the patch for you. You review it, approve it, and it's done. No developer needed for common fixes.
- **Weekly Monitoring** — Instead of monthly, we scan your site every week. Catch regressions faster — before they become problems.
- **Email Alerts on Regressions** — Something broke? You'll know within a day. If a page that was compliant last week fails this week, we email you immediately with the details.
- **Priority Support** — Your support tickets go to the front of the line. Faster responses, faster resolution.
- **PDF Compliance Reports** — Need to show compliance to a client, partner, or auditor? Download a polished, branded PDF report ready to share.

### Who Fortress Is For

- Businesses with 50–500 pages
- Companies that have been threatened with or received an ADA demand letter
- Businesses that don't have a dedicated web developer on staff
- Anyone who wants compliance issues fixed, not just found
- Marketing agencies managing multiple client sites

Fortress bridges that gap. Our auto-fix engine generates code patches for the most common compliance failures:

## How Billing Works

### Monthly Subscription

All CC3PO plans are billed monthly. There are no annual contracts or long-term commitments.

- **Billing date:** Your card is charged on the same date each month (your subscription anniversary)
- **First charge:** Occurs immediately when you subscribe
- **Prorating:** If you upgrade mid-cycle, you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period
- **Downgrades:** Take effect at the start of your next billing cycle

### Cancel Anytime

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard or by contacting us. There are no cancellation fees.

- **When you cancel:** Your monitoring continues through the end of your current billing period

All invoices are generated automatically and available in your dashboard. You can also access them via Stripe's customer portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Do I need to install anything on my website?**
No. CC3PO scans your website from the outside, just like a visitor or a regulator would. There's no plugin to install, no code to add, and no changes to your site until you approve auto-fix patches.

**How long does a scan take?**
Most scans complete in under 5 minutes. Large sites (500+ pages) may take longer depending on the depth of the scan.

**Is my scan data private?**
Yes. Your scan results are visible only to you and your assigned CC3PO team members. We never share your results with third parties. Citadel customers can also configure white-label reports to share under their own brand.

**Do you support websites in languages other than English?**
Our compliance scanning works on any website regardless of language. However, our reports are currently available in English only.

### Plan-Specific

**What's the difference between Shield and Fortress?**
Shield finds problems. Fortress finds them *and* fixes them. Fortress also monitors weekly instead of monthly, sends instant regression alerts, includes priority support, and generates PDF reports. If you want to take action on what's found — not just read about it — Fortress is the right choice.

**What are "industry-specific packs" in Citadel?**
Industry-specific packs add compliance checks tailored to your industry's regulations. For example, healthcare packs include HIPAA-related checks that a general compliance scan wouldn't cover. Financial services packs check for GLBA and FDIC advertising requirements. These packs are configured during onboarding based on your industry.

**What does "dedicated compliance advisor" mean?**
A real person — not a chatbot — who is assigned to your account. They know your site, your industry, and your compliance history. They proactively reach out when regulations change, new vulnerabilities are discovered, or your scan results shift significantly. You can also reach them directly with questions.

**What are white-label reports?**
Reports branded with your company's logo, colors, and name instead of CC3PO's branding. These are useful for agencies, consultants, and enterprises that need to share compliance reports with their own clients or stakeholders.

### Technical

**Will auto-fix patches break my website?**
Auto-fix patches are generated conservatively — we only fix what we're confident about. Before any patch is applied, you review it and approve it. Nothing changes on your live site without your explicit approval.

**What if an issue can't be auto-fixed?**
We provide a clear recommendation explaining what needs to change and why. For complex issues, you can implement the fix yourself, have your developer do it, or contact us for guidance.

**Do you scan behind login walls?**
Standard scans check publicly accessible pages. If you need authenticated scanning (behind a login), contact us — this is available for Fortress and Citadel customers as an add-on.

### Billing & Cancellation

**Is there a free trial?**
Every plan includes a free scan — that's your trial. Run a scan, see your results, then decide if you want ongoing monitoring. No credit card required to start.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard or contact us. No cancellation fees, no hoops to jump through. Your monitoring continues through the end of your current billing period.

**What's your refund policy?**
We offer a full refund within 14 days of your first subscription if you're not satisfied. After that, cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period — no partial refunds for unused days, but you keep access through the period you've paid for.

**Can I switch plans?**
Yes, anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated charge). Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No fees for switching.

**Do you offer annual pricing?**
Not currently, but contact us if you're interested in annual billing — we're happy to discuss it.

### Compliance-Specific

**Does this make my website legally compliant?**
No tool can guarantee legal compliance — any service that claims otherwise is being dishonest. What CC3PO does is identify compliance gaps, help you fix them, and keep you informed about changes. Compliance is an ongoing process, not a one-time checkbox. We make that process manageable.

**What compliance standards do you check against?**
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA (the standard most commonly referenced in ADA legal actions)
- **Privacy:** CCPA, GDPR, state-level privacy laws
- **Security:** OWASP top 10 security headers, TLS configuration
- **SEO:** Core web vitals, meta tags, structured data, crawlability

**How often do compliance standards change?**
Accessibility standards (WCAG) update roughly every 2–3 years. Privacy regulations change more frequently — several US states have enacted new privacy laws in 2023–2025. We monitor these changes and update our checks accordingly. Citadel customers receive proactive notifications about relevant regulatory changes.
