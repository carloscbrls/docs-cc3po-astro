## Signing In

### How Magic Link Login Works

CC3PO uses **magic link authentication** instead of passwords. Here's what that means:

- **No password to create, remember, or reset.** You sign in with just your email.
- When you enter your email, we send a unique, one-time link to your inbox.
- Click that link and you're instantly logged in.
- The link **expires after 15 minutes** and can only be used once.
- We never store passwords — because we never ask for one.

[SCREENSHOT: The login page showing the email field and "Send Magic Link" button]

### Step-by-Step Login

1. **Visit** [comply.cc3po.com](https://comply.cc3po.com)
2. **Enter your email** — use the same email address you signed up with (the one connected to your CC3PO account)
3. **Click "Send Magic Link"** — a confirmation message will appear letting you know the email was sent
4. **Check your inbox** — look for an email from CC3PO with the subject line about signing in
5. **Click the link in the email** — it will open your dashboard in the browser
6. **You're signed in** — no password needed

[SCREENSHOT: The "Check your email" confirmation message after sending the magic link]

### Didn't Receive the Magic Link?

If you don't see the email within a couple of minutes:

1. **Check your spam or junk folder** — email filters sometimes send it there
2. **Make sure you used the right email** — it must match the email on your CC3PO account
3. **Try again** — go back to [comply.cc3po.com](https://comply.cc3po.com) and resend
4. **Add CC3PO to your safe senders list** — this helps future emails arrive smoothly
5. **Still nothing?** Contact us at [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com) and we'll help right away

> 💡 **Tip:** Magic links expire after 15 minutes for security. If it's been longer than that, just request a new one.

This is the big number at the top — your **Living Compliance Score**. It represents how compliant your website is right now across all the standards we check (WCAG 2.2 AA, ADA, EAA).

### Living Compliance Score

Your Living Compliance Score isn't a one-time snapshot — it's a **living metric** that updates every time we scan your website.

**How it works:**
- We scan your website on a regular schedule (weekly for Shield, more frequently for Fortress and Citadel plans)
- Each scan checks your pages against WCAG 2.2 AA standards
- Your score goes up as you fix issues, and down if new violations appear
- The score reflects your **current** compliance posture, not a historical average

**Why it matters:**
- A high, steady score means you're maintaining compliance — not just fixing things after a complaint
- If your score drops, you'll see exactly what changed and can act fast
- Documented, improving scores are your best defense in the event of an ADA lawsuit

## Understanding the Living Compliance Score

The Living Compliance Score is the heart of your dashboard. Here's a deeper look at how it works.

### What Gets Checked

Every scan evaluates your website against **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** standards, which cover:

- **Perceivable** — Content can be perceived by all users (text alternatives, captions, contrast)
- **Operable** — Interface works with keyboard, screen readers, and assistive tech
- **Understandable** — Content is readable and predictable
- **Robust** — Content works reliably across browsers and assistive technologies

These standards align with **ADA** (Americans with Disabilities Act) and **EAA** (European Accessibility Act) requirements.

### How the Score Is Calculated

- Each page on your website is scanned for violations
- Violations are weighted by severity (critical issues have a bigger impact on your score)
- Your overall score reflects the percentage of checks that pass
- The score updates with every new scan — it's always current

### Tracking Progress Over Time

Your dashboard includes a **trend chart** showing how your score has changed across scans. Use it to:

- See if your compliance is trending upward (great job!)
- Spot sudden drops that need immediate attention
- Demonstrate ongoing compliance efforts (important for legal protection)

## FAQ

### How do I log in?

Visit [comply.cc3po.com](https://comply.cc3po.com), enter your email, and click "Send Magic Link." Check your inbox and click the link to sign in. No password required.

### What email should I use?

Use the email address associated with your CC3PO account — the one you signed up with. If you're not sure which email that is, contact us at [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com).

### I didn't get the magic link. What do I do?

First, check your spam or junk folder. Make sure you entered the correct email. Then try sending the link again. If it still doesn't arrive, contact [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com).

### How often is my website scanned?

- **Shield** — Weekly scans
- **Fortress** — Daily scans
- **Citadel** — Continuous monitoring with real-time alerts

### What compliance standards do you check?

We scan against **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** standards, which cover the majority of ADA and EU EAA compliance requirements for websites.

### My score dropped. What happened?

A score drop means new compliance violations were detected on your website. This can happen when:
- You or your team made changes to the website
- A plugin or theme update introduced new issues
- Content was added without accessibility checks

Check the **Issues** section of your dashboard to see exactly what changed, then follow the recommended fix steps.

### How do I improve my compliance score?

Fix the issues listed in your dashboard, starting with the most critical ones. Each issue includes recommended steps. Once fixed, your next scan will reflect the improvement. If you need help, our team can assist — reach out at [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com).

### Can I download my scan reports?

Yes. PDF reports are available in the Scan History section of your dashboard. Shield customers also receive weekly reports by email.

### How do I upgrade my plan?

Contact us at [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com) or visit [cc3po.com/pricing](https://cc3po.com/pricing/) to upgrade to Fortress or Citadel for more frequent scans and advanced features.

### How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime from your dashboard or by emailing [support@cc3po.com](mailto:support@cc3po.com). No contracts, no commitment.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption, don't store passwords (we use magic link authentication), and never share your data with third parties. See the [Security & Privacy](#security--privacy) section above for details.

### What does "Living Compliance Score" mean?

It means your score is continuously updated — not a one-time check. Every time we scan your site, your score reflects your current compliance posture. It goes up as you fix issues and down if new violations appear.
