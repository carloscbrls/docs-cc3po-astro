# 📋 Sales Guide — The Audit Funnel

*Internal document • Updated November 19, 2026*

---

## How Leads Come In

1. Someone visits [cc3po.com/audit/](https://cc3po.com/audit/)
2. They type their website URL + email
3. They get a free Business Health Score scan
4. **You get an email notification**
5. They receive 4 automated emails over 14 days
6. **You call them and close**
7. You fill out the [handoff form](https://offers.cc3po.com/internal-handoff/)
8. Village onboards them automatically

## Call Script

1. **Opening:** "Hi [Name], this is Kathryn from CC3PO. I saw you ran a free Business Health Scan. I've got your score right here — do you have 5 minutes?"
2. **Show the score:** "Your website scored [X] out of 100. The industry average is [62 dental / 58 HVAC]. Here's what we found..."
3. **List top 3 issues** from the scan
4. **The pitch:** "We don't just find problems. We can help you fix them and keep watching the site. A free scan shows where you stand. A $100 strategy call maps the work — and that $100 is credited toward a $495 Fix Sprint if you start within 14 days. From there, Shield keeps monthly WCAG 2.2 AA monitoring and documentation going, and Fortress adds active fixes and priority support."
5. **The comparison:** "A Fix Sprint is $495 one-time. Ongoing monitoring starts at $97 a month for Shield, or $247 a month for Fortress. Citadel is a custom plan from $597 for regulated businesses."
6. **The close:** "Want to see what it looks like? I can get you started this week."

## FAQ Answers

### "How does the AI work?"

"Think of it like a team of specialists watching accessibility, privacy, and related site issues. You don't need to understand the technology — you get the scan, the recommended fixes, and ongoing monitoring and documentation."

### "It's too expensive"

"A part-time specialist adds up quickly. The Fix Sprint is $495 one-time, and Shield starts at $97 a month for ongoing WCAG 2.2 AA monitoring. Fortress is $247 a month if you want us applying fixes and providing priority support."

### "I need to think about it"

"Your website scored [X]/100 — that means [specific issue] is still on the site. A free scan already showed what to fix. I can get you started this week, and the $100 strategy call is credited toward a Fix Sprint if you begin within 14 days."

### "Who is Carlos?"

"Carlos Cabrales is the founder. He has 15+ years in IT, including work on Tesla's Autopilot AI team. He built this system so small businesses could afford the same technology big companies use. He personally oversees every account."

### "What's the refund policy?"

"Month-to-month. Cancel anytime. No contracts. If you're not seeing results in the first 30 days, we'll make it right or you walk away."

### "Will this protect me from a lawsuit?"

"No. CC3PO is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Our monitoring, fixes, and reports help reduce accessibility and privacy risk, but no service can guarantee compliance or prevent a claim. If you've received a demand letter, talk to a licensed attorney."
