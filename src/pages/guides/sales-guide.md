*Internal document • Updated May 8, 2026*

## How Leads Come In

1. Someone visits [audit.cc3po.com](https://audit.cc3po.com)
2. They type their website URL + email
3. They get a free Business Health Score scan
4. **You get an email notification**
5. They receive 4 automated emails over 14 days
6. **You call them and close**
7. You fill out the [handoff form](https://offers.cc3po.com/internal-handoff/)
8. Village onboards them automatically

## Call Script

1. **Opening:** "Hi [Name], this is Kathryn from CC3PO. I saw you ran a free Business Health Scan. I've got your score right here — do you have 5 minutes?"
2. **Show the score:** "Your website scored [X] out of 100. The industry average is [62 dental / 58 HVAC]. Here's what we found..."
3. **List top 3 issues** from the scan
4. **The pitch:** "We don't just find problems. We fix them. Automatically. Our team monitors your website 24/7, answers your reviews, posts your social media, keeps you compliant, and watches your competitors. It's like having a whole department for a fraction of the cost."
5. **The comparison:** "If you hired separately, you'd pay $14,000/month. CC3PO gives you all of that for $497 to $1,297 a month."
6. **The close:** "Want to see what it looks like? I can get you started this week."

## FAQ Answers

### "How does the AI work?"

"Think of it like a team of specialists. One watches your website 24/7, one handles your reviews, one creates your content, one keeps you compliant. They all work together and learn your business over time. You don't need to understand the technology — you just see the results every morning in your inbox."

### "It's too expensive"

"Compare it to hiring one person. A part-time social media manager alone costs $2,000/month. For $497, you get website monitoring, review management, social media, AND compliance. It's actually 90% cheaper than doing it the old way."

### "I need to think about it"

"Your website scored [X]/100 — that means [specific issue] is costing you customers right now. Every day that goes unfixed is revenue walking out the door. I can get you started this week and you'll see results in the first month. No long-term contract."

### "What's the refund policy?"

"Month-to-month. Cancel anytime. No contracts. If you're not seeing results in the first 30 days, we'll make it right or you walk away."
