## What Is the CC3PO Compliance Scanner?

The CC3PO Compliance Scanner is a free tool that audits any public website across **7 critical compliance categories** in about 30 seconds. It gives you an overall compliance score (0–100) and a detailed breakdown of every issue found, with fix recommendations.

[SCREENSHOT: Scanner homepage with URL input field and "Scan Now" button]

### What It Checks

| Category | What It Covers |
|----------|---------------|
| **Accessibility (WCAG)** | Whether your site meets accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1) that ensure people with disabilities can use it. Checks alt text on images, color contrast, keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, form labels, heading structure, and more. |
| **Privacy** | Whether your site has a privacy policy, how it handles personal data, and whether it meets requirements under laws like CCPA and GDPR. Checks for privacy policy presence, PII handling, and data collection practices. |
| **Cookies** | Whether your site discloses cookies and trackers to visitors, has a cookie consent banner, and respects opt-out preferences. Detects tracking cookies, analytics pixels, and third-party trackers. |
| **SEO** | Whether search engines can find and understand your site. Checks meta tags, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, heading hierarchy, and mobile-friendliness. |
| **Speed** | How fast your site loads for visitors. Measures server response time, page weight, image optimization, render-blocking resources, and caching. |
| **Security** | Whether your site protects visitor data with HTTPS, keeps software updated, and avoids common vulnerabilities. Checks SSL certificate, mixed content, and known vulnerability patterns. |
| **Security Headers** | Whether your site sends browser security headers that prevent attacks like clickjacking, code injection, and data theft. Checks for headers like Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, Strict-Transport-Security, and others. |

## Understanding Your Compliance Score

Your overall score is a weighted average across all 7 categories. Here's how to think about it:

- **90+** — You're in great shape. Small fixes can push you to 100.
- **70–89** — Solid foundation, but gaps exist. Focus on Critical issues first.
- **50–69** — Significant problems. A compliance plan is strongly recommended.
- **Below 50** — Urgent attention needed. Legal and security risks are real.

### Why Compliance Matters

- **Accessibility** — Laws like the ADA (US) and EAA (EU) require websites to be usable by people with disabilities. Non-compliance can lead to demand letters and lawsuits.
- **Privacy** — Regulations like CCPA and GDPR require clear disclosure of data practices. Violations carry heavy fines.
- **Security** — A breach or vulnerability can cost your business thousands in damages and lost trust.
- **SEO** — Poor SEO means fewer customers find you on Google.
- **Speed** — Slow sites lose visitors — 53% of mobile users leave a page that takes over 3 seconds to load.

## Understanding Each Category

### Accessibility (WCAG)

WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) is the global standard for web accessibility. The scanner checks your site against WCAG 2.1 criteria, including:

- **Alt text** — Images need descriptions for screen readers
- **Color contrast** — Text must be readable against its background
- **Keyboard navigation** — Every feature must work without a mouse
- **Form labels** — Every form field needs a visible, programmatically associated label
- **Heading structure** — Pages need a logical heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- **ARIA attributes** — Dynamic content needs proper accessibility markup

### Privacy

Checks whether your site:

- Has a visible privacy policy
- Discloses what personal data it collects
- Provides opt-out mechanisms
- Follows CCPA/GDPR data handling requirements

### Cookies

Checks whether your site:

- Has a cookie consent banner
- Discloses all cookies and trackers to visitors
- Allows users to reject non-essential cookies
- Identifies third-party tracking (analytics, ads, pixels)

### SEO

Checks whether your site:

- Has proper meta tags (title, description)
- Has structured data (schema.org markup)
- Has a sitemap.xml and robots.txt
- Uses proper heading hierarchy
- Is mobile-friendly

### Speed

Checks whether your site:

- Responds quickly (server response time under 600ms)
- Loads efficiently (total page weight)
- Uses optimized images
- Avoids render-blocking resources
- Implements proper caching

### Security

Checks whether your site:

- Has a valid SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- Avoids mixed content (HTTP resources on HTTPS pages)
- Keeps server software updated
- Doesn't expose known vulnerability patterns

### Security Headers

Checks whether your site sends these protective HTTP headers:

| Header | What It Prevents |
|--------|-----------------|
| Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS) | Forces HTTPS connections |
| Content-Security-Policy | Prevents code injection attacks |
| X-Frame-Options | Prevents clickjacking |
| X-Content-Type-Options | Prevents MIME-type sniffing |
| Referrer-Policy | Controls how much referrer info is shared |
| Permissions-Policy | Controls which browser features are allowed |

## FAQ

### Is the scan really free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no credit card, no trial. Just enter a URL and scan.

### What websites can I scan?

Any publicly accessible website. Private/intranet sites, password-protected pages, and localhost addresses cannot be scanned.

### How long does a scan take?

Most scans complete in about 30 seconds. Larger or slower sites may take slightly longer.

### What do you do with my email address?

If you enter your email for a report, we send you one detailed report for that scan. We do not spam, sell, or share your email. See our [privacy policy](https://cc3po.com/privacy) for full details.

### Is my scan data private?

Yes. Scan results are not publicly accessible and are not shared with third parties.

### Can I scan my client's website?

Absolutely. Many web designers, agencies, and consultants use the scanner to audit client sites. You can also use the `?url=` parameter to send clients a direct scan link.

### What's the difference between the free scan and paid plans?

The free scan gives you a one-time snapshot. Paid plans provide **ongoing monitoring, automatic fix patches, alerts when new issues appear, and dedicated support** — so you stay compliant without constantly checking.

### Do I need technical knowledge to understand the results?

No. Every issue is explained in plain language with a clear fix recommendation. If you can read this guide, you can read the scan results. For hands-off help, our paid plans include automatic fixes.

### Can I share my scan results?

Yes. You can share the `?url=` link to let someone else run the same scan, or forward the email report.

### What WCAG standard does the scanner check against?

The scanner checks against **WCAG 2.1**, which is the most widely adopted version and the basis for most accessibility laws worldwide.

### I got a low score. What should I do first?

1. Filter issues to show only **Critical** severity
2. Focus on **Accessibility** and **Security** categories first (these carry the most legal and safety risk)
3. Review each issue's fix recommendation
4. If you need help, consider a CC3PO plan — we fix issues for you

### Is the scanner affiliated with any government agency?

No. CC3PO is an independent compliance monitoring service. Our scanner follows published standards (WCAG 2.1, OWASP, etc.) but is not a government tool or certification body.
