# CC3PO Stories — Complete Customer Guide

Turn your memories, stories, and the people you love into something you can hold forever. CC3PO Stories creates personal songs, memoirs, immersive places, and more — all from the stories you tell us.

---

## Quick Start

1. Go to [stories.cc3po.com](https://stories.cc3po.com)
2. Browse the 6 service cards on the homepage
3. Click a service that speaks to you (e.g., "Personal Song")
4. Read about the service, see what's included, and choose a package
5. Click the **"Create Their Song"** / **"Tell Your Story"** button
6. Your email opens to stories@cc3po.com with a pre-filled subject line
7. Tell us about who or what you want to preserve
8. We respond, and together we create something beautiful

That's it. No account needed, no forms to fill out — just an honest conversation.

---

## What Is CC3PO Stories?

CC3PO Stories is a service that turns real conversations into lasting keepsakes. You talk — by voice, text, or video — and we listen. Then we create something beautiful from your words: a song, a memoir, a soundscape, a letter, a memorial, or a dream journal.

This isn't a template. This isn't AI-generated filler. Every creation is unique, drawn from your real stories, and belongs entirely to your family.

## FAQ

### How do I get started?

Visit [stories.cc3po.com](https://stories.cc3po.com), choose a service, and click the call-to-action button. Your email will open to stories@cc3po.com. Tell us a little about what you'd like to create, and we'll take it from there.

### Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no account to create, no signup form, no password to remember. Just send us an email and we start talking.

### What if I'm not sure which service is right for me?

That's completely okay. Email us at [stories@cc3po.com](mailto:stories@cc3po.com) and tell us what you're thinking about — a person, a place, a feeling, a milestone. We'll help you figure out which service (or combination) is the best fit.

### Can I create something for someone who has passed away?

Yes. Many of our services are specifically designed for this — especially the **Life Story Book**, **Living Memorial**, and **Sensescape**. You share what you remember, and we create from your memories. We'll guide you through it gently.

### How does the interview work?

We sit down with you or your loved one by phone, video call, or in person (depending on location). It's a real conversation — not a questionnaire. We ask the questions a good friend would ask, and we listen for the stories that matter. The length depends on the service and package you choose.

### Can I give this as a gift?

Absolutely. A personal song or life story book makes a deeply meaningful gift for birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's Day, Father's Day, or just because. Email us and we'll help you set it up.

### How many revisions do I get?

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds:

| Package Type | Revisions |
|-------------|-----------|
| Entry tier | 1 revision round |
| Mid tier | 2 revision rounds |
| Top tier | 3 revision rounds |

Additional revisions can be arranged if needed — just ask.

### Who owns the final product?

**You do.** Every creation belongs entirely to your family. We don't add songs to a catalog, reuse melodies, share stories, or train AI models on your content. It's as personal as a fingerprint.

### What if I need something faster than the listed timeline?

Reach out to us directly at [stories@cc3po.com](mailto:stories@cc3po.com) or call us at **(209) 232-1265**. We'll do our best to accommodate rush orders, especially for time-sensitive occasions like memorials or milestones.

### Do you work in Spanish / other languages?

Yes. CC3PO is a Latino-owned business and we're comfortable working in Spanish and English. If you prefer to tell your story in Spanish, we'll listen in Spanish and create in the language that feels right.

### Is my information secure?

Yes. Your stories, letters, and memories are stored securely and privately. We don't share them, sell them, or use them for anything other than creating your personal keepsake. Time Capsule Letters are encrypted and held until the delivery date you chose.

### What if I want to cancel my Dream Journal subscription?

You can cancel anytime — no penalties, no hoops to jump through. Just email us at [stories@cc3po.com](mailto:stories@cc3po.com) and we'll take care of it. Your existing dream logs and artwork remain yours to keep.

### Can I combine services?

Absolutely. Many families create a Life Story Book alongside a Personal Song, or add a Sensescape to a Living Memorial. Email us and we'll help you put together a package that tells the whole story.

### How do I contact you?

- **Email:** [stories@cc3po.com](mailto:stories@cc3po.com)
- **Phone:** (209) 232-1265
- **Website:** [stories.cc3po.com](https://stories.cc3po.com)

We're a real team, and we respond personally. No chatbots, no hold music — just people who care about your story.
