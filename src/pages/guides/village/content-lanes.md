# Content Lanes

CC3PO publishes to **Instagram and Facebook**. Content moves in lanes, and the approval rule is different in each one. Work out which lane a post belongs to *before* drafting anything.

## The lanes

- **The main lane (draft and approval).** Posts are drafted, shown to Carlos as the **exact final text with the actual media**, and publish only after he approves what he saw. A "yes" to a summary or a description is not approval. A substantive edit to his copy gets listed explicitly and confirmed in his own words.
- **The content lane (a standing grant).** In the dedicated content lane, CC3PO content may post without asking first — the grant covers CC3PO content only. Personal content stays out of this lane, with the single exception Carlos named himself, handled in his own words and with the care rules below.
- **The replies lane.** Simple replies — short thank-yous, warm greetings, confirming already-public facts — may send without prior approval, and every sent reply is logged (who, where, exact text, when). Anything beyond simple is drafted for approval. Doubt means it isn't simple.
- **The fantasy desk.** Daily pieces publish and report after; the weekly flagship still requires Carlos's approval of the exact text.

## Rules every post follows

- **Tie it to something real** — an album's themes, Central Valley life, the systems people live inside. Never content for content's sake. Skip a post rather than force one.
- **Verify before you state.** Release dates, links, and availability are checked on the platform itself before anything is announced. Never invent lyrics, dates, links, or claims.
- **Heavy material carries its resources.** Posts touching the heaviest themes carry the crisis line — call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text **TALK to 741741** (Crisis Text Line) — checked at scheduling time, not after.
- **Some lanes are paused by Carlos's word.** When a lane is paused, nothing in it is drafted, queued, or scheduled until he reopens it. A pause is a rule, not a suggestion to route around.
- **Verify after publishing.** Read the post back on the platform and confirm the caption, media, and account match what was approved. A successful publish command is not proof — the readback is. Report the live link, and say plainly when a post did not go out. Never post a duplicate to fix a doubt; check first.

## The education series — "Plain Language"

A running series that rebuilds concepts in plain language, using the Village's own work as the example. Its rules are the strictest in the building, and they double as this library's style standard:

- **Rule Zero — define everything from zero.** Never assume the audience knows a tool or product name. Concepts before acronyms, always. If a name appears, its job is explained in the same breath.
- **Simplicity rule.** A graphic carries **one idea** in as few words as possible (aim for roughly 40 words of body text). The detail lives in the caption. Big type, breathing room.
- **Trademark rule.** Every graphic carries the CC3PO wordmark as bold live text in the footer band with the academy's address. Text is always composited in the layout — **never baked into generated art**; generated visuals must be text-free.
- **The public-safety line.** Examples use first names and jobs only — never internal addresses, machine names, keys, or client details.
- **Posting grant.** For this series, posts built from examples Carlos feeds the Village may be built and posted at the Village's judgment, with live links reported after. Every other lane keeps its own gate.
